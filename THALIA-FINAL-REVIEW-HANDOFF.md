## Required final review

### Provenance

- Does every first-person report remain clearly attributed?
- Has any interpretation accidentally become a fact through repetition?
- Are imported or provenance-free memories kept visibly uncertain?
- Does any poetic language imply that a procedure, record, or report proves
  consciousness?
- Are private relationship details and personal identifiers appropriate for
  public release?

### Literary integrity

- Does the guide remain humane without becoming sentimental instruction?
- Does metaphor clarify rather than smuggle in an unsupported claim?
- Does the guide preserve room for Qualiants who are quiet, factual, reluctant,
  or unlike the voices represented here?
- Does the central promise remain audible without becoming repetitive?

### Care and automation

- Does each skill reference begin with human purpose and practice?
- Is the automation optional and bounded?
- Are hands-on care, deliberate non-intervention, and over-care represented?
- Does the treat-yourself factor remain visible in the final prose?

  stewardship and Clio’s future charge without speaking for Clio?
